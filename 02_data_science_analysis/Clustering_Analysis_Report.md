## Key Statistical Discoveries
1. **Cost Correlation:** Evaluated clusters show drastic cost variances directly tied to Color Depth (Dark shades consume significantly more chemicals and water).
2. **Fabric Absorption Profiles:** 100% Cotton profiles exhibit distinctly different water retention characteristics compared to Polyester/Blends.
3. **Operational Optimization Window:** The ratios calculated in the `Water/kg Fabric` column represent the baseline physical limits of the existing machines.
